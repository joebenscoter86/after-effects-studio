## Description

Create and revise editable motion graphics in Adobe After Effects with plain-language direction.

After Effects Studio combines native AE control with a detailed motion-design library developed through real production work. It guides composition, construction, timing, typography, camera continuity and visual review while adapting to your brand and chosen style.

Build animated diagrams, titles and explanatory sequences; refine an existing composition; inspect rendered frames; and retain native layers, keyframes and text for further editing.

Free at launch for personal and paid client motion work, including delivery of editable AE projects. Joe's original plugin and motion library cannot be repackaged or resold as a standalone product under the selected license. The initial route targets local Codex desktop on macOS, licensed After Effects 2026 and Node.js 24+. One separate-Mac rehearsal passed on macOS 26.6.2 with AE 26.5x89, Node 26.5.0 and Codex desktop 26.930.61225; other combinations are not yet verified. Physical product animation may require supplied artwork, footage or models. Generated assets from other services are separate.

## Starter prompts

- Create an editable explainer in After Effects using my brand colors.
- Improve the timing and transitions in my current composition.
- Build a title sequence, show me a preview, then help me revise it.
