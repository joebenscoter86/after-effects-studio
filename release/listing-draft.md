## Description

Create and revise editable motion graphics in Adobe After Effects with plain-language direction.

After Effects Studio combines native AE control with a detailed motion-design library developed through real production work. It guides composition, construction, timing, typography, camera continuity and visual review while adapting to your brand and chosen style.

Build animated diagrams, titles and explanatory sequences; refine an existing composition; inspect rendered frames; and retain native layers, keyframes and text for further editing.

Free at launch for personal and paid client motion work, including delivery of editable AE projects. Joe's original plugin and motion library cannot be repackaged or resold as a standalone product under the selected license. Requires a supported local After Effects installation and compatible agent environment. Physical product animation may require supplied artwork, footage or models. Supported operating systems, AE versions and installation requirements will be stated with the verified release. Generated assets from other services are separate.
