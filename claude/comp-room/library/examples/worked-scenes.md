# Worked motion studies

These studies retain the measured sequence and implementation decisions from the Content builds while removing private assets and fixed brand styling. They are recipes to adapt, not completed media or proof that an adaptation is approved. Use the user's fonts/palette, the comp's dimensions, native exact text, and appropriate licensed/supplied art.

## B. Congestion → intervention → visible capacity

Source mechanism: house-polish scene 02, about 5.4s at 30fps. Star: a device with a measured blank screen. Change: an exact metric responds to an action. Adapt to storage, processing, inventory or another real bounded quantity.

1. Measure the screen after asset preparation. The source cut-out was 972×601; do not reuse its screen geometry for a different image.
2. Introduce device, guides, native label, track, bar, number and unit progressively over roughly 20–40f.
3. Stagger incoming items 3f, about 8f travel each, as the bar fills and count decreases. End layers at their intended boundaries. One slider/data source drives numeric text.
4. On the limit word, a ring identifies remaining capacity. A tiny localized spring adds emphasis without shaking the entire composition.
5. An indicator arrives, anticipates, then dives into the screen over about 5f. Scale relative to its resting size.
6. Items fan out with 2f stagger and about 9f travel, then collapse over 3f. Bar and count move together over 26f. Ensure both describe the same quantity: used and free move in opposite directions.
7. A corner badge appears after resolution, followed by a readable hold. The indicator returns quietly. Optional 3–4% carrier drift sustains the chosen style.

Verify metric source and units; the original study's 1GB/312GB is not transferable product evidence. Use a screen matte/bezel, check type at maximum zoom, and inspect the bad state, intervention peak and result. A real screenshot may replace the device metaphor when it provides clearer evidence.

## C. Carry real evidence across a context change

Source mechanism: CONNECT scene-v 9. Star: an actual selected reply; environments: source workspace and destination draft. General technique for transfers, references, synchronization and hand-offs.

### Three composed poses

- Start: enough source context to identify the selection. Define precise source crop, pixel scale, pivot and selection treatment.
- Action: the same object is detached and carried while the environment turns/rearranges. Make the middle legible and avoid toolbar seams. Movement is editorial unless actual recording proves it.
- Payoff: match destination bounds, position, scale, angle and background before changing which layer carries the pixels. Preserve the required source/destination relationship.

### Details that mattered

Hide duplicate destination text while the carried copy is in flight. Keep authentic source content distinct from a simulated product bubble: an instruction is not a response. If Multiply leaves a pale rectangle, inspect alpha and place the intended backing inside the precomp. Keep reduced source context long enough to identify origin. Remove decorative arcs that explain nothing.

Tie phases to real spoken cues. Complete the action on exact contact, then release attention to the next subject. Inspect detachment, maximum rotation, overlap, near-contact and hand-over. A good-looking transfer does not establish that a message was sent.

The reusable idea is an identifiable object crossing a purposeful spatial change. Private text, screens, marks and footage do not ship. Use supplied evidence or a labeled fictional example. Historical approval of the original scene does not approve this adaptation.

## D. A collected resource reaches its use

Use this as a storyboard for a causal transfer, not a required water aesthetic. A 12-second rain-to-garden explainer can move from catchment to use without making three unrelated cards:

1. **Collect.** Rain enters one named collector. Show its contained water level or another visible accumulated state. Keep the collector on screen long enough to identify where the resource lives.
2. **Depart.** Lower that contained level as one identifiable water volume or droplet leaves. Do not fade the whole collector at the same moment and call it drainage. If the container must leave later, keep it visible until departure is legible.
3. **Carry.** Animate that same volume through a channel or matched cut. If a line depicts the route, its near end meets the outlet, its far end meets the receiving area, and the payload stays on that route at the start, midpoint and approach to contact. A line floating above the droplet is decoration, not the transfer. A simple shared frame is preferable when a more elaborate transition breaks registration.
4. **Contact and response.** Let the payload meet the soil or plant before the growth cue begins. Then hold the changed result with the final copy readable. A newly appearing droplet at the garden is not the same carried object unless the cut visibly matches its position/shape and timing.

**Choose one honest route.** Before drawing a channel, mark the source outlet `S` and recipient contact `R` in comp coordinates at the transfer pose. If using a straight channel, place its endpoints at `S` and `R` and key the payload through sampled positions on that same segment. For a curved channel, sample the actual route and place the payload on those measured points; a separate plausible-looking line is not enough. Inspect the delivered movie at departure, one-quarter, midpoint, three-quarters and contact. The payload should overlap the visible route at every sampled point, and the route must still meet both objects. If this cannot be made true, **remove the channel entirely** and show a direct moving payload from `S` to `R` in one shared frame or a visibly matched cut. Keep the collector present long enough to see the departure and delay growth until after contact. An unattached guide that remains after the collector fades fails the causal scene even if the water level falls and the plant grows.

Inspect delivered frames around source departure, route midpoint and recipient contact, plus full-speed playback. If any one pose breaks, revise the geometry or timing before adding a guide, easing or secondary motion. The same source-depletion / carried-identity / recipient-response test applies to a file moving between apps, energy feeding a device or parts joining a product; the literal water art does not.
