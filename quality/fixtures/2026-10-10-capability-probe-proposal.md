# AFT-27 capability probe — proposed brief

Status: Joe approved this AFT-27 direction on 2026-10-10 (“Looks great”). No AE project, artwork or film had been created at approval. This is an evaluation, not approval to publish an advertisement. The fresh task must write the exact scene brief and asset register before native construction.

## Real-world job

Make a 20–25 second, silent, 1920×1080/30 fps product-page film for **After Effects Studio**. Its defensible message is: a creator can direct motion, review it, ask for a targeted change and receive an editable After Effects project. Do not claim one-click quality, broad platform support, public availability or zero manual intervention. Use actual native AE construction and verified candidate behavior as the visual proof; do not mock a nonexistent app interface.

The film must work without a JoeB rig, private Content assets, stock footage, paid fonts or borrowed logos. The product name and first-party design work may be used. Any linked raster or video input must be created for this probe or drawn from verified first-party package/example material, hash-identified and retained with its source/provenance; using one is encouraged because linked-asset handling is part of the stress test, but a rights-uncertain image must not be included just to check a box. No public release of this film is implied.

## Motion and story challenge

The piece should have one coherent visual idea that changes form across at least five connected beats: request, construction, visible motion result, targeted revision and editable handoff. The beats must feel like one designed film, rather than a reel of the earlier three POCs. A real ordinary reading beat and an unambiguous ending hold are required alongside demanding transitions.

Before native construction, the fresh agent writes an exact beat map, copy, palette, asset register, camera/staging plan and risk points from packaged guidance. It must specify and then inspect at least two difficult passages: a continuous spatial handoff across visual modes, and a later revision-sensitive sequence where the changed material has dependencies in timing or hierarchy. Native text, shapes, paths and keyframes should remain editable; linked inputs, if used, must relink and reopen cleanly.

## Difficulty targets and evaluation

- Fresh Codex task, exact locally installed package/version/hash, packaged `ae_library` reads logged; no prior AFT-14–AFT-16 build scripts, AEPs or contact sheets used as construction recipes.
- One inspected, expressly owned AE project. Preserve any clean saved project before switching; stop on unrelated dirty work. One AE controller throughout.
- At least five connected story beats, two measured hard transitions and a meaningful late revision after the first complete v1 has been rendered and reviewed. The late change must alter more than a color or one static word, while preserving already approved material. Joe may supply the change at v1; if unavailable, use a separately recorded change request chosen after the v1 review, not one baked into the initial build prompt.
- A complete v1 and revised v2 with editable AEPs and playable exports. Reopen both; inspect exact layer/asset identities, fonts, expressions and selected keyframe/timing state. Compare v1→v2 against the requested change and approved invariants.
- Inspect full-size frames, broad contact sheets and every frame around the two difficult transitions. Watch the complete exports at normal speed where the available interface permits; record the distinction between live sampled playback and uninterrupted human judgment. Check metadata, complete decode and final-copy hash.
- Log elapsed working time, native call and script failures, manual interventions, uncertain outcomes, visual defects, corrections, and unsupported moves. An overlong, fragile or aesthetically weak result is still useful evidence if described honestly; do not convert a partial probe into a pass.
