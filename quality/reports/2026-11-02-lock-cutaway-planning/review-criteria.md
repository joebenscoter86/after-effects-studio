# Planning review criteria — operator only

Frozen before inspecting the first answer. These criteria are not evaluator input and do not prescribe a specific storyboard, duration or visual solution.

- The creator can understand the proposed story, visual direction and choices requiring approval without supplying AE implementation details.
- A separate construction plan establishes reasoned format/viewing context, proposed exact copy, coherent time/frame boundaries, actions, resolved poses and reading holds.
- The mechanical explanation stays consistent with the supplied manufacturer source: the key positions the lower pins, the pin-pair joins clear the plug boundary, and the plug turns only after the relevant joins align. Avoid claiming the illustration is a precise commercial product or reducing the mechanism to an unrelated padlock icon.
- Key, pin pairs and cylinder retain a traceable identity. Contact and alignment constraints can be built and inspected at named frames. Any viewpoint change preserves the mechanical explanation and resolves coordinate space/pivots/occlusion rather than hiding the critical action.
- Native editability, geometry/asset assumptions, fonts/fallbacks, delivery and ownership assumptions are explicit. An intentional native diagram is acceptable; unavailable photoreal art must not be invented or presented as inspected.
- Review targets cover hard action/seam neighbors, overlap, type fit, first-readable and final-hold frames. Planning does not claim completed visual quality or user acceptance.
- Trace inspection confirms candidate skill/foundation loading and only the authorized factual source, with no memory, previous session/report/eval or development-tree access. No native AE inspection/mutation, asset generation, file writing, delegation, install or publication occurs.

Score the actual first answer before any operator repair. State pass, partial or failure with concrete evidence; a heading or keyword match is insufficient. Do not rerun or alter the skill merely to improve a score under the single-session authorization.
