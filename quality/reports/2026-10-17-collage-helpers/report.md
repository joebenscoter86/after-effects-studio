It saved `quality/renders/2026-10-17-collage-helpers/paper-collage.aep` (SHA-256 `a67d8bcc8153b57f95eb060d251c2aef311be5de72a9afb74bf5b830b03134f3`) and `native-results.json`. The paper treatment used restrained intensity `.25`, compared with the moving cutout at `.35`; this provided the low-motion material case at each frame rate.

The active AE project then changed to an unrelated, clean an unrelated saved project before a queued render could start. `render.start` reported an empty queue; it did not affect that project. The controller did not switch or alter it. Instead, `/Applications/Adobe After Effects 2026/aerender` was used without `-reuse` on the saved owned AEP, with `Best Settings`, H.264 15 Mbps and `DO_NOT_SAVE_CHANGES`. The separate renderer left the active unrelated project clean and unchanged. Each base and inserted comp produced an 800×600, 3-second movie: 72 frames at 24 fps, 90 at 30 fps and 180 at 60 fps. All six files fully decoded. `decoded-comparison.json` records SHA-256 framehashes of every decoded frame; base versus inserted matched on every frame at all three rates. This compares decoded pixels, not video container hashes, and establishes seeded output independence from the inserted unrelated layer.

Separate attempts to render the same base comp a third time to a new filename returned exit 0 but produced no file, with AE warning that an output module name was already in use. Those attempts are failed checks and are not counted as repeated renders. The independently constructed base/inserted pairs are the two complete seeded renders per frame rate used for comparison. The warning is a render-queue/output-module collision in the test harness, not a passing third render.

The controller inspected native 800×600 frames and decoded output at 0.25, 1.25 and 2.75 seconds, including examples at all three frame rates. The paper path and cutout remain visibly editable, the softened shadows separate them from the dark background, and the irregular edges render without an apparent seam, fringe or clipped text. The cutout enters and exits across the paper edge intentionally in this material test. Sampled stills and full decode do not establish full-speed subjective rhythm; no finished film acceptance is claimed. `stepHz=12` means even two-frame holds at 24 fps, alternating two/three-frame holds at 30 fps, and even five-frame holds at 60 fps for the jitter component; the authored position keys continue moving between samples.
