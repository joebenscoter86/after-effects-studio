# Guidance gaps and assumptions

- The skill explains realistic-asset requirements and truthful factual inputs, but does not include a ceramic-specific lighting recipe, real grinder asset, renderer compatibility matrix or asset-acquisition capability. The lighting and capture treatment use ordinary production knowledge.
- 1920×1080 at 24 fps is provisional because the prompt gives duration only. Output format remains a user input before production.
- Actual model, price, currency, product media and brand assets are missing. No product appearance, model name or commercial price was invented.
- AE version, supported 3D renderer and media capabilities are unknown because AE inspection is prohibited. Native 3D import/render capability must be checked before choosing that route.
- A real camera orbit requires actual captured footage or faithful 3D geometry. A single image can only support a revised camera treatment, not reveal unknown sides truthfully.
