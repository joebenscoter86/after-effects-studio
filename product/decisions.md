# Product decisions

- D-001 — Build a universal motion plugin: user-defined subject and style, with the production-derived motion language as the default craft foundation.
- D-002 — Pursue a full public v1 release. Demonstrate capability through complete examples, editable output and meaningful revisions.
- D-003 — Launch free; decide monetization later. Do not add billing or paid tiers for v1. Free use does not decide redistribution licensing.
- D-004 — Develop in this independent After Effects Studio repository. Four workspaces: product, plugin, quality and release. This structure was approved by Joe in the originating task.
- D-005 — Maintain AGENTS.md as the canonical map; CLAUDE.md is a pointer. The plugin's bundled library is its single maintained craft source.
- D-006 — Launch publicly through a Git-backed Codex plugin marketplace with a guided install. Pursue the universal-directory route for local AE access in parallel; directory confirmation is not a prerequisite for the Codex launch. Git is the package source, not a required creator skill. This choice does not authorize repository publication, outreach or submission.
- D-007 — Scope the initial v1 host to local Codex desktop on macOS with After Effects 2026. Advertise only the exact OS, AE, Codex and Node builds that pass candidate acceptance; validate Node 24 before retaining it as the minimum. Windows, older AE releases and other hosts require separate evidence before support claims. One genuinely separate Mac is the portability gate and Joe may operate it; that run is not novice-usability evidence.
- D-008 — License Joe's original plugin additions and motion library for free personal, organizational, client and commercial motion creation. Permit ordinary installation, updates, private adaptation and delivery of rendered or editable motion outputs without output credit. Reserve redistribution, repackaging and resale of Joe's original plugin/library as a standalone product. Preserve the upstream MIT runtime and dependency rights separately. The exact grant is in `../license.md` and the installable `../plugin/license.md`; final legal and asset review remains a release gate.
- D-009 — Use JB Technology LLC as the public plugin publisher and developer identity, per Joe's direction. This identifies who publishes and supports the release; it does not itself transfer copyright in Joe's original materials. The final product name is After Effects Studio. Use Joe's existing `joebenscoter86` GitHub account for the public repository and its Issues as the initial support intake. A custom domain and dedicated support email are not launch prerequisites. Public website, privacy, terms and support URLs still require an actual owned repository/page before they enter release metadata. This choice does not authorize making the repository public.
- D-010 — Treat the type-led identity and causal explainer as acceptable proof-of-concept examples. Before considering public v1 finished, run a separate, substantially harder creative and control capability probe. Its brief, difficulty targets, editable output, full-speed visual review, revision fidelity, practical limits and Joe's assessment must be recorded. This does not require running the probe immediately or imply that the POCs demonstrate the upper limit.
- D-011 — Do not transfer or install the AFT-18 test kit on Joe's company-managed Mac. Its security and software policy are not ours to waive. Continue local build, package, creative and release-preparation work without using that machine. Keep independent-machine installation as an open release gate; use a personally owned or explicitly authorized separate Mac when one becomes available. Developer-Mac or isolated local checks are useful rehearsal evidence but do not satisfy AFT-18, and no public-install or general-availability claim may be inferred from them.
- D-012 — Close AFT-14–AFT-17 against their scoped creative, editable-delivery and portable-guidance evidence without waiting for AFT-9's naturally occurring uncertain-write timeout. Joe approved this dependency correction after the three-style comparison. AFT-9 remains a separate release reliability gate and continues to block AFT-18 and AFT-23. The accepted proof-of-concept films do not establish the harder AFT-27 capability boundary, clean-machine portability or public-release readiness; recorded pacing limits remain visible in their evidence.

- D-013 — On 2026-10-13 Joe approved the revised path-forward review and detailed Linear handoff. Replace AFT-9's naturally occurring timeout wait with a repeatable controlled possible-write failure and real native-state inspection, paired with a successful control; no deliberate AE hang or blind replay. Remove AFT-9 as a prerequisite for AFT-18; both remain prerequisites for final Codex candidate review. This supersedes D-012's AFT-9 → AFT-18 edge while retaining completed creative acceptance. Split Codex release (AFT-24/AFT-25) from directory eligibility, preparation and submission (AFT-30/AFT-35/AFT-36); directory waits do not block Codex launch. AFT-20 prepares reviewed publisher/support pages; AFT-31 completes concrete source/asset/license review; AFT-32 publishes reviewed infrastructure only after specific authorization and verifies live destinations before final release review. Add lifecycle safety (AFT-33) and actual public-Git install rehearsal (AFT-34). Freeze the candidate and revalidate affected scenarios rather than repeating every accepted film. One eligible separate Mac remains required; Joe may operate it. A speculative hosted bridge, more showcase films, outside technical tester, Windows expansion, billing and a custom domain are not v1 prerequisites. The detailed contract is release-execution-plan.md and current Linear descriptions. Approval to revise planning does not authorize outreach, repository/page/release publication, directory upload/submission or publication.

Open: an actual verified public repository/page for the chosen destination, specific rights questions found in AFT-31, exact tested release versions, access to an eligible separate Mac, and whether/how the local AE integration can enter the universal directory. No public publication or outreach is implied by these decisions.
