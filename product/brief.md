# Product brief

After Effects Studio is a free-at-launch plugin for creators and small creative teams who want to create and revise editable motion graphics through plain-language direction. It combines local native AE control with a detailed motion language developed through Joe's Content production work.

The user's subject and brand define the visual look. Bundled guidance supplies progressive construction, causal action, timing, camera continuity, typography, asset preparation and rendered review. The optional JoeB preset controls appearance only.

The first useful experience is: install, connect to an existing or blank AE project, describe a scene, inspect a preview, request a meaningful revision and retain editable native work. The user must not need the private Content repo, Joe's fonts or media, a personal rig or a Higgsfield subscription.

V1 includes native creation/revision, the complete portable craft foundation, guided setup, clear recovery and verified examples. Billing and paid tiers are deferred. Licensed Adobe software and a compatible agent host remain prerequisites. External artwork or generation may be necessary for a faithful physical product scene.

Full public release is the target. Current evidence demonstrates packaged knowledge and native build/frame control on one Mac. Clean-machine onboarding, complete video delivery, broader platform support and premium creative quality remain release work.
