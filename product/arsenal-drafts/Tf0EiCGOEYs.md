## A pill button built in its own comp
id: 01-pill-button-in-own-comp
status: proven (accepted by Joe 2026-11-21: rebuilt in After Effects at 9:16 on generated artwork from this recipe alone, reviewed against the tutorial's result and phone MP4s sent; he said "i approve")
source: Tf0EiCGOEYs 36.0–153.0 s
jobs: lines and shapes; type; layout and repetition; workflow
what it does for a beat: The call-to-action button for an end card or app demo: a flat, fully rounded pill with a centred caps label, kept in its own comp so the scene can place, scale, slide and "press" it as one layer.
what it looks like: A saturated blue pill, about four times as wide as it is tall, with half-round ends and a short two-word all-caps label in heavy white sans set with very wide letter spacing, centred in the pill. Flat: no stroke, no gradient, no shadow at this stage. The comp around it is white and leaves a wide margin on every side.
ingredients:
- Composition (Composition > New Composition; Composition Settings dialog) — built-in
- Rectangle Tool shape layer (Rectangle Path, Fill, Stroke) — built-in
- Horizontal Type Tool text layer with the Character and Paragraph panels — built-in
- Font Montserrat ExtraBold — free (Google Fonts); any heavy geometric sans stands in
steps:
1. The scene this button goes into is a square comp (shown f002492: Project panel lists it). 
   - Width × Height: 1080 × 1080 px (shown f002492)
   - Frame Rate: 60 fps; Duration 0:00:20:30 (shown f002492)
2. Make a new comp for the button (shown f002492: Composition Settings dialog open over the empty scene). 
   - Width: 800 px, Height: 800 px (shown f002561 crop: Height being typed as 800 with Width already 800 px; the 800 × 800 comp is listed in f003793)
   - Pixel Aspect Ratio: Square Pixels; Frame Rate: 60; Duration: 0:00:20:30; Resolution: Third (shown f002561)
   - Background Color: White (shown f002561). The comp's background colour does not render when the comp is nested; it only makes the white page visible while working (inferred).
3. With the Rectangle Tool, drag a rounded box roughly across the middle of the comp (shown f003793: a rounded rectangle path with handles, Contents > Rectangle 1 > Rectangle Path 1). 
   - Size at that point: 631.8, 165.9 (shown f003793)
   - Roundness: 451.0 (shown f003793). This is far more than half the box's height, so After Effects clamps it and the ends become full semicircles (inferred; the pill in f005653 has half-round ends).
   - Rectangle Path Position: 0,0; Transform: Rectangle 1 Position 0,0, Scale 100 %, Rotation 0 (shown f003793)
4. Turn the stroke off and the fill on (shown f004816: Fill 1 has its eye on, Stroke 1's eye is off; toolbar Fill is a solid swatch and Stroke shows the red "none" slash; in f003793 it was the reverse, a 10 px white stroke and no fill).
5. Set the fill colour in the Shape Fill Color dialog (shown f004816: dialog open at 000000; the hue is then dragged into blue).
   - Fill Color: #0036FF (R 0, G 54, B 255; H 227°, S 100 %, B 100 %) (shown f005051; OK pressed with the same values at 85.5 s, scratch frame cut from source.mp4)
6. Type the rectangle's size exactly (shown f005653).
   - Size: 700.0, 170.0 (shown f005653)
7. With the Type tool, click and type a two-word all-caps label; centre the paragraph (shown f006459: Paragraph panel with Center text selected; the first, oversized letters spill past the pill).
   - Font: Montserrat, style ExtraBold (shown f007013 crop)
   - Size: 60 px; Leading 0 px (shown f007013 crop and the 123 s cut; it does not change a single-line label); Fill colour white, no stroke (shown f007013 crop)
   - Vertical and horizontal scale 100 % (shown f007147)
   - Tracking: 157 (shown f007147 mid-drag at 84; settled at 157 from 121.5 s on, crops cut from source.mp4 at 121.5–123 s; the timeline's "157" reading of the tracking field is the same)
8. Move the label into the middle of the pill (shown f007013: text Position 394.3, 424.0; then f007876: 399.1, 421.0 with the Align panel open, "Align Layers to: Composition"). Whether the author used the Align buttons or nudged by hand is not visible (inferred: aligned to the comp's centre, since the measured label centre sits on the pill centre within a pixel).
9. Resize the comp around the button in Composition Settings (height down from 800, width up from 800), leaving a margin (shown f008145: Height scrubbed to 531 with Width 800; the 140 s cut from source.mp4 shows 1034 × 531; final size in f009158).
   - Final comp size: 1040 × 530 px (shown f009158 crop of the Project panel)
   - Every layer kept its place relative to the comp's centre: the label's Position went from 399.1, 421.0 to 399.1, 286.5 (−134.5, half of 800 − 531) and then to 519.1, 286.0 (shown f007876, f008145, f009158). So the comp was resized about its centre, After Effects' default anchor (inferred).
   - The margin left is 170 px each side and 180 px above and below the pill (inferred from the figures). The next tip's soft shadow reaches about 140 px below the pill and fits inside this margin (see layout).
layout: Measured on the result frame f009525 (comp 1040 × 530 shown at 72.4 %, calibrated on the comp's white edges, 634–1386 × 120–503 on screen). Pill: 700 × 170 comp px (measured 498–507 screen px wide), so 67.3 % of the comp's width and 32.1 % of its height, centred both ways (measured). Placed at 100 % in a 1080 square scene it is 64.8 % of the picture's width and 15.7 % of its height (inferred from the figures; recipe 03 measures exactly that). Ends: full semicircles. Label: cap height 44 comp px, 25.9 % of the pill's height (measured; consistent with 60 px Montserrat); width about 505 comp px, 72 % of the pill's width (measured, 828–1193 on screen in f009158); centred on the pill within 1 px horizontally and vertically (measured). Letter gaps are wide: tracking 157 adds 0.157 em, about 9.4 px at 60 px, to every letter gap (inferred from the setting); the gap between the two words reads about one letter wide (measured by eye on f009525). Colour as rendered in the recording: pill about (52, 42, 252), label white about (253, 253, 253). The typed value is #0036FF; the recording reads more violet, the wide-gamut display shift described in the lessons, so build with #0036FF. No stroke, no shadow, no gradient on anything at this stage.
timing and easing: none given (nothing animates in this tip).
variations: none given.
pitfalls: none given. Seen on screen: the first comp is far too big, so the button is resized after the label is set (step 9), and the label's position shifts with each resize because After Effects keeps layers centred.
longest on: Drawing and styling the pill (51–95 s, about 44 s: draw, stroke off, fill on, colour dialog, exact size), then the label (95–129 s, about 34 s, most of it on tracking and position), then the comp resize (129–153 s, about 24 s) and making the comp (36–51 s, about 15 s).
build notes: Comp: app.project.items.addComp(name, 1040, 530, 1, 20.5, 60) (white bgColor only for viewing). Shape layer: comp.layers.addShape(); Contents add "ADBE Vector Group" > "ADBE Vector Shape - Rect" with "ADBE Vector Rect Size" [700,170] and "ADBE Vector Rect Roundness" 451 (any value of 85 or more gives full round ends); add "ADBE Vector Graphic - Fill" with "ADBE Vector Fill Color" [0, 54/255, 1]; no stroke group. Layer at the comp's centre [520, 265]. Text: comp.layers.addText("…"), TextDocument font "Montserrat-ExtraBold" (PostScript name; check app.fonts or substitute a heavy geometric sans and match cap height 44 px), fontSize 60, fillColor [1,1,1], applyFill true, applyStroke false, tracking 157, justification ParagraphJustification.CENTER_JUSTIFY; then centre the layer on the pill by its sourceRectAtTime bounds (the author's Position was 519.1, 286.0 with the anchor at the baseline). Building straight at 1040 × 530 skips steps 2 and 9 and gives the same picture.
result: start=158.0 end=159.4 still crop=634,120,753,384 frame=f009525
before: f002492 (the empty square scene and the new-comp dialog)

## A soft coloured lift under a button, and an inset label

jobs: shadows and light; depth and finish
what it does for a beat: Lifts a flat UI button off a white page so it reads as a pressable object, with a long, very soft glow-like shadow in the button's own colour straight underneath, and gives the label a slight pressed-in feel with two tiny shadows.
what it looks like: A blue pill button on white with a wide, soft blue haze spreading mostly downward (strongest just under the pill, gone about one pill-height below it), and a faint halo on the sides and top. The white caps label has a hairline dark edge under each letter and a barely visible soft light haze above it.
ingredients:
- Drop Shadow — built-in; Effect > Perspective > Drop Shadow (shown f009406: found by typing "drop" in Effects & Presets, under Perspective)
- The pill button of recipe 01 (figures repeated below so this recipe stands alone)
- Font Montserrat ExtraBold — free (Google Fonts)
steps:

   - Opacity: 50 % (shown f010792; the effect's default, left unchanged)

   - Distance: 48.0 (shown f010792)
   - Softness: 248.0 (shown f010792)

   - Shadow Color: black (shown f011307 swatch; the hex is never opened; black is the effect's default)
   - Direction: 0x +180.0° (shown f011307; passed through 188° while dragging, f011211)
   - Distance: 4.0 (shown f011589; it was 5.0 in f011307)
   - Softness: 0.0 (shown f011307)
   - Opacity: 40 % (shown f013475; it stayed at the default 50 % until 3:44, typed at f013424)
6. Apply Drop Shadow to the label a second time; After Effects names it "Drop Shadow 2" and it starts at the effect's defaults: black, Opacity 50 %, Direction 135°, Distance 5.0, Softness 0.0, while the first one reads 180° and 4.0 (shown in cuts from source.mp4 at 198.2, 198.7 and 199.2 s). Turn it into a soft light from above.
   - Direction: 0x +0.0° (straight up) (shown f012151; dragged from 135° through −2°, f011999)
   - Shadow Color: #FFFFFF (shown f012259: dialog reads H 0, S 0 %, B 100 %, R/G/B 255, FFFFFF)

layout: Measured on the result frame f013607 (the button comp at 50 %, calibrated on the comp's white edges, 750–1269 × 179–443 on screen; comp px = 2 × screen px). Pill 700 × 170 at the comp's centre (67.3 % × 32.1 % of the comp), as in recipe 01; label cap height 44 px, centred. The pill's shadow (traced to the pill: it starts at the pill's edge, follows its outline and is the pill's own blue): straight below the pill it reads about (161, 168, 251) on a (253, 253, 253) page 2 px under the pill's bottom edge, (200, 205, 250) about 50 px under, (236, 240, 251) about 100 px under, (250, 252, 254) about 140 px under, and is gone by about 150 px, so it fades out over roughly 0.85 of the pill's height (measured). Only red and green drop; blue stays near 252, so it reads as a blue haze, not a grey shadow. To the sides it spreads about 60–80 px past the pill's ends (measured at the pill's centre line, x 870 to about 945), and above the pill a faint tint (about 245 on 253) reaches some 25 px (measured). Recording colours: pill about (52, 42, 252) against the typed #0036FF; the wide-gamut shift in the lessons applies, so aim for the typed value and compare darkness by the red/green drop.
timing and easing: none given (nothing animates).

The effects act on the layer's own alpha, so the pill's shadow follows the half-round ends.
