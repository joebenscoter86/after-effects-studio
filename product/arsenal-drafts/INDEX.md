# Arsenal menu: 53 checked recipes from four videos

## How to use this

Pick the section for what the beat needs, scan the one-line entries, then open the recipe in `<video id>.md` under its number.
A recipe is listed under every job it serves, so the same entry can turn up in several sections. Each section starts with the entries most likely to be useful.
`unproven` means the recipe has not yet been built in After Effects and compared with the tutorial's own result. Treat every value as a starting point until it has been.
The last three sections are planning notes for whoever improves the library: duplicates to merge, shared parts, and where the recipes are weakest.

## Taught more than once

These groups are the plan for merging. The recipe texts stay as they are for now.

1. **Graphic-textured particle drift**: YBeMmC43Q1U #10, il_OcNyqDOE #07.
   - Shared: a solid carries CC Particle World, set to Textured QuadPolygon with Texture Layer pointing at a small graphic. The emitter moves to one side and gravity drops close to zero, so the pieces drift sideways instead of bursting upward.
   - Differs: #10 uses Direction Axis physics with short life (Longevity 1 s, Velocity 1.08, Gravity 0.03, Producer X −0.58, Radius Y 0.225) and leaves the default sizes. #07 uses Twirl with long life (about 29 s, Velocity 0.91, Gravity 0.02, Extra 0.8, Producer X −0.82, Radius Y 0.245), particles that grow with age (Birth 0.15, Death 1.5), and the scene grade pasted onto the particle layer. #07 also records its finished values (Radius Y 1.365, Gravity 0.002), which make a full-height emitter. #10 shows the leaves over a window view; #07 sends them across a whole landscape.

## Gaps the recipes share

These are counted over the 53 recipes from their `unknowns`, `result` and `build notes`. One recipe can have several gaps.

| Gap | Recipes | Where it bites |
|---|---|---|
| Sizes not given: comp size, element diameters, font size, positions of most layers | 21 | Lines-and-shapes and layout recipes from YBeMmC43Q1U, and every shape build in 8oVLS0ZlKC4 |
| No `layout` line: written before shares of the picture were required | 25 | Every recipe in YBeMmC43Q1U; the other three videos all have one |
| Finished motion not given: what animates, end values, keyframe ease, real playback speed, or a playback never seen | 18 | Orbit and carousel nulls, the tile slide, sway, grass, puppet and hair motion, the merge and split keys |
| Steps or values that need the interface: hand-dragged curve points, gradient stops, layer-style menus, puppet pins and object-matte painting, per-face material overrides, modifier-drags, camera menu commands, viewer layouts | 20 | Curves (5), puppet and matte (4), material overrides (3), gradients (2), gestures and menus (6) |
| No clean result frame: the mouse, handles or labels sit in every frame, the window is shorter than half a second, or the motion cannot be judged in the crop | 19 | Results have to be masked or compared by value rather than by pixels |
| Colours only sampled from compressed video, or not given | 15 | Backdrops, shape fills, the fog colour, tint sources |
| Steps done off screen or across a jump cut, never shown being made | 10 | A switched-off effect, a duplicated glow, a changed gradient, a pasted stack, a reordered layer |
| Match names or menu indexes unconfirmed (dropdown order, enum values) | 12 | Particle, text-selector, light and grain dropdowns |
| Blend mode or opacity of a layer never shown, although the look depends on it | 5 | Particles, shadows, dot grid, light shapes |

The weakest point is the gap between the written steps and the finished motion. Most recipes give their settings in full, but about a third never show how the finished piece moves. Second is size: 25 recipes have no measured layout and 21 are missing basic sizes. Third is the material that cannot be scripted. Curves points and puppet pins come up again and again, so a library of saved presets would close most of that gap.
