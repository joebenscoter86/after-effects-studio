## Edge-only light outline from a solid logo
id: 01-edge-only-outline-from-logo
status: proven (accepted by Joe 2026-11-26: rebuilt in After Effects on generated artwork from this recipe alone, reviewed against the tutorial's result and phone MP4s sent; he said "Accept all six")
variant of: 04-light-sweep-edge-on-buttons (tYRvoI04pJk #04)
differs: Shared: the same mechanism, CC Light Sweep used as an edge-only light with Sweep Intensity 0, Light Reception Add and Edge Thickness 4 (the parent's sweeps 1 and 4 use 4 as well, step 7), starting from Edge Intensity 50 as in the parent's step 5. Here the effect sits on a solid-colour logo, with a black Fill effect above it so the inside disappears and only the lit edge remains; in the parent it sits on a rounded-rectangle shape layer that keeps its own dim 4 px stroke and gradient fill, and there is no Fill effect. Here one sweep only; in the parent four sweeps are stacked (steps 6-7). Here Shape is Sharp, Width 79, Edge Intensity 90, Direction 14 degrees, Center 960,540 (the comp centre), Light Color white; in the parent Shape is Smooth, Width 140, Edge Intensity 25 and 7, Direction 0 and 180, Center 2564.0, 612.0, Light Color AFB0FF (steps 2-4, 7). Here the result is a still logo rim on black, with the layer's blending mode set to Add at one point and Normal afterwards (unknown whether on purpose); the parent has no logo, no blend-mode change and adds a Scale and Size bounce on a pop-in (steps 10-12). Here the lit edge is part of a logo shown on black; the parent lights a button where a glowing line meets it.
source: Nh3TenxxQzQ 87.5–160 s
jobs: lines and shapes; glow and colour; type
what it does for a beat: Turn a solid-colour logo into a thin glowing rim line with nothing inside it, as the base for an animated outline reveal.
what it looks like: On black, a white logo shape is hidden and only a hairline of light remains along the edges that face the light direction: the dipped top edge of the body between its two shoulders, and the leaf above it, fading out along the curve. The rest of the silhouette stays black.

ingredients:
- Logo with transparent background, imported and nested in a second composition — built-in (import; said "import that composition into a new one", frames f005888 and f006664 show a Main comp holding a nested logo comp)
- Fill — stock; Effects & Presets search "fill", under Generate (shown f006664)
- Layer blending mode Add — built-in (shown f007544)
- CC Light Sweep — stock; Effects & Presets search "cc light", under Generate (shown f008792)

steps:
1. Bring in the logo (a white shape, transparent background, 1920×1080, 30 fps source) as its own composition, then put that composition as the single layer of the main composition (shown f005888, f006664). The comp's own background is black (shown f007070).
2. Drag Fill onto the logo layer (shown f006664, drop in progress; effect in panel f007070).
   - Color: black (said; swatch black shown f007070). Fill Mask None, Opacity 100 % (shown f007070).
   - Why: the inside must not show, only the edge (said). The logo now vanishes into the black comp.
3. Set the layer's blending mode to Add (said; shown f007544, Mode column reads Add). Note: later frames (f007676, f008792, f010340) show the Mode column back at Normal, so the build that follows runs with Normal. Add matters only when there is a non-black background behind the layer (said that it clears the black area); on the all-black comp shown it would make no visible difference (inferred). Whether the author reverted it on purpose is not said (see unknowns).
4. Drag CC Light Sweep onto the same layer, below Fill (shown f008792, Fill listed first).
   - Sweep Intensity: 0 (said; shown f008792). Only the edge light is wanted (said).
   - Center: 960,540, the comp centre (said "960 by 540"; shown f010340). Before this it read 960,546 then was moved (shown f008792 → f009120 range).
   - Edge Intensity: 50 at default (shown f008792), then raised to 90 (said "increase its intensity"; shown f009120 and f010340).
   - Width: 50 at default, raised to 79 (shown f009120 and f010340). The narrator says "edge" for this raise; the Width readout is what changed on screen.
   - Edge Thickness: 4.00, left at default (shown f008792, f010340).
   - Shape: Sharp; Light Color: white; Light Reception: Add — all defaults left alone (shown f010340).
5. Turn the Direction dial to place the lit edge. The narrator first says to set it to zero and then to something near that (said); the author scrubs it and settles on 14° (shown f010340, "0x+14.0°"). Direction is the property that is animated next (said, technique 02).
   - Direction: 0x+14° (shown f010340)

layout: The picture below is the viewer at 50 %, 960×540 px for the 1920×1080 comp; all measured on f006664/f010340.
- Logo silhouette (before it is blacked out, f006664): about 23.5 % of the picture width wide and 52 % of the height tall (about 450 × 560 comp px), centred horizontally and sitting slightly below the vertical centre (its middle at about 0.50 of the height). On f010340 that arc runs from about 426 to 566 px of the 960 px picture width (about 14.6 % of the width, about 140 px at 50 % view, about 280 comp px), brightest at the bottom of the dip (picture x about 505, y about 205–213 of 540) and fading to nothing toward both ends and slightly beyond each shoulder, on both sides of the dip. variations: A background other than black can be used; then the Add mode on the layer keeps the black fill from showing (said). More edges and colours are added in the next technique.

build notes: Add the layer to a comp over a black background. Effects in order: Fill (Color black), CC Light Sweep (match name believed "CC Light Sweep"; set Center 960,540; Direction 14°; Shape Sharp; Width 79; Sweep Intensity 0; Edge Intensity 90; Edge Thickness 4; Light Color white; Light Reception Add). ## Three coloured edge sweeps that spin in and meet at one angle
id: 02-multi-colour-edge-lines-converging
status: proven (accepted by Joe 2026-11-26: rebuilt in After Effects on generated artwork from this recipe alone, reviewed against the tutorial's result and phone MP4s sent; he said "Accept all six")
variant of: 04-light-sweep-edge-on-buttons (tYRvoI04pJk #04)
differs: Shared with the parent: stacked CC Light Sweep copies made with Ctrl+D on one layer, each set to Sweep Intensity 0 so only the edge line shows, Light Reception Add, and Edge Thickness 4 on the thin sweeps (parent steps 3, 4, 5, 7; here step 2). Here a bare logo layer with a black Fill effect above the sweeps carries them, so the logo body is invisible and the edge outlines the logo; in the parent the sweeps sit on a rounded-rectangle shape layer with a gradient fill and a dim 4 px stroke. Here Edge Intensity is 90, Width 79 and Shape Sharp; in the parent the thin sweeps use Edge Intensity 25, Width 140 and Shape Smooth, with thick faint copies (Edge Intensity 7, Thickness 20, parent steps 6 and 7) that the parent infers give the soft wash. Here three copies carry three Light Colors (white, violet CC00FF, orange) on Directions 14, 251 and 118 with Center left at the comp centre 960,540; in the parent all four share the pale violet AFB0FF, Directions are 0 and 180 (two copies each) and Center is moved to 2564,612 (and 2573.6,1215.2 on the bottom copy). Here Direction is keyed from 0 s to about 5 s (2 turns, 0 degrees; easy ease; a speed-graph reshape that the recipe says is not previewed); in the parent no keys on the sweeps are given and the animation is a bounce expression on Scale and rectangle Size. The result here is outline slivers sweeping round a logo and converging, with no card, no bounce and no glow at this stage.
source: Nh3TenxxQzQ 160–259 s
jobs: lines and shapes; glow and colour; energy and impact
what it does for a beat: Reveal a logo as thin bright outlines that start at different angles, race around it and lock together at one point. Use it when a logo needs a sleek outline intro before the fill or glow arrives.
what it looks like: On black, a logo (white artwork whose Fill effect is set to black, so its body is invisible) shows only thin slivers of its outline in white, violet and orange. At the start white sits on the top edge (leaf underside and top dip), violet on the left-lower side and orange on the lower right. When the keys play, the slivers sweep clockwise round the contour, and at the end they stack on the same top edge, blending to a pale pink-white.
ingredients:
- CC Light Sweep — stock; Effect > Generate > CC Light Sweep (shown f011064: the search "cc light" lists it under Generate). The narrator calls the result an "edge" effect; the screen shows CC Light Sweep, so the screen wins.
- Direction keyframes with Easy Ease and the Graph Editor's speed graph — built-in
steps:
1. Start from the white logo layer on a black comp, 1920×1080, 30 fps, 20 s long (shown f012400 timeline/time display). The logo layer already has a Fill effect above the sweep, its Color set to black (shown f009686, swatch in the Fill's Color row), which is why the body is invisible and only the sweep lines show. 2. Add CC Light Sweep to the logo layer. (shown f009686: effect present in Effect Controls; menu path inferred)

   - Direction: 14° (shown f012400, settled 0x+14.0°; f009686 reads 76.0° mid-change and is not cited)
   - Shape: Sharp (shown f009686)
   - Width: 79 (shown f009686)
   - Sweep Intensity: 0 (shown f009686) — so only the edge line shows, not the broad sweep
   - Edge Intensity: 90 (shown f009686)
   - Edge Thickness: 4.00 (shown f009686)
   - Light Color: white (shown f009686 swatch)
   - Light Reception: Add (shown f009686)
   The narrator talks about Direction here; Width 79 and Edge Intensity 90 were raised from 50 a little earlier (previous technique, per the timeline: he says he raises the edge intensity), so treat all the values listed as the intended settings, read off the panel (shown f012400).
3. Duplicate the effect (said: duplicate; the Ctrl+D shortcut is shown f010487 as an on-screen caption). The copy is named CC Light Sweep 2 (shown f012400). (said, shown f010487)
   - Direction on copy 2: 251° (shown f012400)

6. Move to about 5 s (display reads 0:00:05:02 at 30 fps, i.e. 5 s 2 frames, shown f013228; narrator says "around five seconds"). ## Four stacked Glow effects blooming a thin outline
id: 03-stacked-glow-on-outline
status: proven (accepted by Joe 2026-11-26: rebuilt in After Effects on generated artwork from this recipe alone, reviewed against the tutorial's result and phone MP4s sent; he said "Accept all six". The accepted fifth build was replaced by a sixth from the one extra round he approved. differs: Shared: both stack stock Glow effects on thin bright line art on a near-black ground, made by adding one Glow and duplicating it so the copies carry the first one's settings, then raising Glow Radius per copy so a tight halo and a wide faint bloom add up; both leave Glow Based On Color Channels, Composite Original Behind, Glow Operation Add and Glow Colors Original Colors at their defaults (parent step 3). Differences: here there are four Glows at radii 50, 165, 480 and 1000 (steps 3 to 7; Glow 4's start from 480 is inferred), where the parent's section gives radius 39 and then 242 for the one glow it ever shows, and the parent infers that the other keeps 39 and never shows the duplicate (parent step 4, unknowns). Here the Glows sit on the outline layer itself, below its three CC Light Sweep effects, where the parent puts Glow on an adjustment layer above the graphic (parent steps 1 and 2). Here the duplicate is made with Ctrl+D (said; the resulting Glow stack is shown f017242, f018351, the keypress is not), where the parent says the shortcut is neither said nor shown (inferred). source: Nh3TenxxQzQ 259–313 s
jobs: glow and colour; shadows and light
what it does for a beat: When a thin bright outline on black needs to read as lit from within, with a tight hot edge, a near halo and a wide soft haze. One Glow is flat; several at growing radii give the falloff.
what it looks like: A thin white curve with a pink-magenta end, on near-black. A hot white core hugs the curve, a soft grey-white haze spreads a few hundred pixels around it, and a very faint wide wash fills much of the picture. The curve's top end is pink-magenta and blooms into a clear magenta wash about 100 px across (in a 930-px crop) around the top of the arc and over the upper tail; the haze on the left of the arc is grey-white.

ingredients:
- Glow — stock; Effect > Stylize > Glow (said "glow effect", shown in the Effects & Presets search f016107, list under Stylize)
- Duplicate effect (Ctrl+D on the selected effect) — built-in; said "press control D", the stack of Glow, Glow 2, Glow 3, Glow 4 is shown f018351
- Layer being lit: a logo outline layer on black, already animated with three light sweeps (see the earlier technique that draws the outline; here it is only the source). The Glows sit below the sweeps in the effect stack, shown f016107 and f018351.

steps:
1. Have the outline layer selected, with its sweep effects already in place, black ground. (shown f015553: no glow yet, thin partial arc only)
2. Drag Glow from the Effects & Presets panel (searched "glow") onto the layer. It lands below the three sweep effects. (said; the drag is in progress at f015960, the landed Glow with its defaults is shown f016107)
3. On Glow, change three values; the rest stay at their defaults. (said for radius, threshold and intensity words; screen decides values)
   - Glow Based On: Color Channels (shown f016107, left at default)
   - Glow Threshold: 60 % → 24 % (said "24"; shown f017242 reads 24.0 %; the default 60.0 % is shown f016107)
   - Glow Radius: 10.0 → 50 (default 10.0 shown f016107; said "50"; shown f017242 reads 50.0)
   - Glow Intensity: 1.0 → 1.3 (shown f017242 reads 1.3; narrator's "1.3" is said; default 1.0 shown f016107)
   - Composite Original: Behind; Glow Operation: Add; Glow Colors: Original Colors; Color Looping: Triangle A>B>A; Color Loops: 1.0; Color Phase 0; A & B Midpoint 50 %; Glow Dimensions: Horizontal and Vertical (all shown f018351 and f017242, unchanged defaults)
4. With Glow selected, press Ctrl+D to duplicate it. The copy ("Glow 2") keeps threshold 24, intensity 1.3. (said "press control D"; the result is shown, Glow and Glow 2 listed f017242; the keypress itself is not shown)
5. Glow 2: Glow Radius 165 (said; shown f017995 reads 165.0). The narrator's purpose: a little glow reaching outside the outline.
6. Duplicate again; Glow 3: Glow Radius 480 (said "480 or 500, whatever"; shown f018351 reads 480.0 with threshold 24.0 and intensity 1.3).
7. Duplicate again; Glow 4: Glow Radius 1000 (said, "so that it's very bright"; shown f018505 reads 1000.0 and threshold 24, intensity 1.3). The duplicate carries Glow 3's 480 (inferred; no kept frame shows Glow 4 at 480), and the field is being retyped at f018157/f018268 (not landed).

- The visible arc is a left-hand crescent about 19–21 % of picture height tall (about 105–115 of 542 px, y≈315 to 430 in the 1080 frame band; measured), peaking at the left near the centre line; a thinner wavy tail runs right from its top and a short separate pink fragment sits lower right.
