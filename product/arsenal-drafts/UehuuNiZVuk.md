## Round-capped line that draws on and then wipes off (Trim Paths)
id: 01-trim-paths-travelling-round-cap-line
status: proven (accepted by Joe 2026-11-26: rebuilt in After Effects on generated artwork from this recipe alone, reviewed against the tutorial's result and phone MP4s sent; he said "accepted")
source: UehuuNiZVuk 21.9–159.0 s
jobs: lines and shapes; energy and impact
what it does for a beat: A designer reaches for this when a plain stroke should grow along its path and then have its tail chase the head off the far end, as a quick graphic accent or as the building block for a larger line design.
what it looks like: One short, horizontal, flat-ended-then-rounded olive/mustard stroke on black, centred in the comp. It grows from its left end to the right, and before it has finished growing its tail starts to leave from the left, so a capsule-shaped piece appears to travel left to right and vanish at the right end. Both moves are eased.

- Shape layer with Pen tool path, no fill, one stroke — built-in; Pen tool (shown f000928), Fill/Stroke swatches in the toolbar (shown f002461)
- Stroke Line Cap = Round Cap — built-in; Contents > Shape > Stroke 1 (shown f001828)
- Trim Paths — built-in; layer "Add" menu > Trim Paths (said; the menu itself is not on screen in a kept frame, so the path is inferred)
- Keyframe Assistant > Easy Ease (F9) — built-in (shown f003975, menu open with Easy Ease F9)

## Two travelling lines turned into an X by duplicating and rotating

jobs: lines and shapes; layout and repetition; energy and impact
what it does for a beat: A designer reaches for this when one animated stroke (see recipe 01-trim-paths-travelling-round-cap-line) should become a symbol or accent such as a cross, a close mark or a plus-style hit, by reusing the same animation on turned copies.
what it looks like: Two thin dark-teal round-ended strokes on a plain bright teal ground cross at the middle of the picture to form an X.
