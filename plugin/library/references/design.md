# Design before motion

Premium motion comes from a coherent visual idea, well-made assets, and deliberate choreography. Start with what changes for the viewer: a file becomes organized, a cost becomes visible, a system becomes understandable. A decorative object entering the frame is not automatically a story beat.

## Scene contract

For each section capture: intended understanding; anchor object; initial and final states; primary action; supporting details; title; source of factual text/numbers; spoken cue or time; exit/hand-off. Maintain one visual center of gravity. Establish a hierarchy of headline, hero and supporting evidence. Check the composition with all motion stopped.

## Material and hierarchy

Use a restrained palette with semantic color roles. Choose a display face and supporting face that remain readable at delivery size; measure actual rendered bounds. Hold essential text long enough to read. Respect destination UI and crop safe areas appropriate to the platform and format; don't blindly reuse 1080x1920 coordinates elsewhere.

Use native shapes for precision: construction, selection, bars, diagrams, connectors, masks and text. For realistic objects, use strong supplied or generated assets with consistent lighting, viewpoint and material. Separate any piece that needs independent motion. Render shadows in AE when possible so light remains consistent. Generic icons are acceptable if the brief calls for iconography; they are a failure when they stand in for a crafted material object the design requires.

## Choreography

Build a picture over time: scaffold, primary object, meaningful detail, state change, resolution. Retain a spatial anchor through transformations. Stagger supporting parts so the eye can follow causality. Contrast a smooth build with a crisp snap when the meaning turns. Holds may drift subtly when that helps the look; stillness is also a valid intentional choice.

## Reference study

Measure a short reference shot: frame rate, object positions, scale, spacing, colors, overlap and the strongest frame. Reconstruct a study where authorized; compare aligned frames side by side; then apply what it teaches to an original scene. Template statistics describe that template sample, not all professional animation or audience response.
