# Paper collage in motion

Read `ae_library` documents **motion-language**, **production-workflow**, **brief-template**, then **paper-collage-style** and **paper-collage-recipes**. This skill works on its own; do not assume motion-studio ran first or hand the task back to it. Read **paper-collage-examples** for a worked treatment. Load **paper-collage-jsx** only when using the native helpers. If library retrieval is unavailable, these same bundled files are at `../../library/language/core.md`, `../../library/language/production-workflow.md`, `../../library/templates/brief.md`, `../../library/styles/paper-collage.md`, `../../library/recipes/paper-collage.md`, `../../library/examples/paper-collage.md` and `../../library/scripts/paper-collage.jsx` relative to this skill directory.

Define the viewer takeaway and brief before choosing material. Treat paper collage as cut edges, overlap, shadow depth and purposeful hand-like movement; palette, subject and type are user choices. Use a few legible pieces with a clear visual hierarchy. Keep native text and guides crisp and readable. Load typography guidance only when animated type is an important part of the request; the paper style does not require it.

Choose assets honestly. Native shapes and paper cards can make a complete abstract collage without a generator or supplied media. They cannot faithfully depict a particular real person, product or photograph. Use rights-cleared supplied cutouts when the brief needs that specificity; optional generation is a separate route when tools and permission are available. Verify alpha edges, resolution, source rights and consistent lighting before treating external art. Default to editable AE text for labels. When photographic paper makes clean text look overlaid, try the native ink treatment in `paper-collage-recipes` and inspect the render. If the requested realism still fails, the recipe's optional handwritten-paper plate can bake visible lettering into distinct paper assets while retaining exact Source Text as the copy record. Disclose that visible copy needs regeneration and relinking after edits; it is not live editable typography. Check exact spelling and rendered bounds; no specific third-party font is assumed.

For a native-only tactile brief, build the primary sheet with `AESCollage.paper` or a comparably tested native edge/surface/shadow stack. Keep its contour visible against the ground and retain a backer, seam or joining piece through the final invitation. Read the type-led announcement pattern in `paper-collage-examples` when the request combines a named event with animated text. A smooth flat polygon plus an offset shadow is a blockout if the requested material is paper. In the final hold, put the full event name and invitation on distinct measured text-safe regions; no foreground sheet, fold, tape or accent may cover any settled glyph. Inspect the start and end of that hold in the delivered movie at full size, then a small viewing-size preview. Revise layer order or geometry when either complete string is not clear.

Before building all beats, render one final-size material proof of the main sheet over its actual backdrop. Look inside the paper for stock variation and at its perimeter for a legible cut edge, contact shadow and softer lift shadow. If those cues disappear at playback size or after H.264 export, correct the material while the scene is still simple; flat polygons with decorative tape do not satisfy a tactile-paper brief. Then render the final hold and confirm the same material cues remain visible around the complete text. Reserve enough tool/time budget for this proof and the final export.

Inspect the exact AE project and discover operations with ae-control before native writes. `AESCollage.paper` makes an editable native card; `treatCutout` modifies only an explicit owned, two-dimensional non-text layer. Preserve preexisting expressions and unknown keys. A conflict is a stop-and-inspect result, not permission to clear a layer. Choose `stepHz` and seed deliberately; moving cutouts may step while labels remain smooth. For a calm treatment set intensity to zero, remove flicker/boil and needless camera movement, and keep text motion independently controlled. This is a creative variant, not an accessibility certification.

Inspect overlap, edges, shadows, seams, alpha halos, read order and the actual rendered frames. Review at full speed where available. Record what was rendered and what remains a taste judgment; technical success and user acceptance are separate.
